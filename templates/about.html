🩸 About Our Project

The AI-Based Blood Bank Management System is designed to connect blood donors, hospitals, and patients through a single platform.

The system helps users search for donors, make emergency requests, register hospitals, generate reports, and receive AI-based donor recommendations.

This project was developed using Python, Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and Artificial Intelligence technologies.
